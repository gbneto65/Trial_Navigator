# 🧪 Trial Navigator

**Right-sized trials for real-world impact. Drive adoption. Lift sales.**

Trial Navigator is an interactive statistical decision-support tool designed to help researchers, technical teams, and business leaders design trials that generate reliable conclusions while maximizing commercial impact.

Unlike traditional power calculators that focus exclusively on statistical significance, Trial Navigator combines **scientific trial design** with **business-oriented success criteria**, enabling users to quantify both statistical confidence and the probability of achieving meaningful commercial outcomes. 

---

## Why Trial Navigator?

A statistically significant result is not always a commercially meaningful result.

Many field trials fail to demonstrate value because they are:

- Underpowered
- Overly variable
- Designed around unrealistic expectations
- Focused only on p-values instead of business outcomes

Trial Navigator helps users determine:

✅ The probability of achieving a meaningful business result

✅ Statistical power for the planned trial

✅ Required sample size to reach target power

✅ Impact of variability on trial success

✅ Impact of multiple endpoint corrections

✅ Risk of false negatives before conducting the trial

---

## Key Features

### 📈 Commercial Success Probability

Estimate the probability that:

```text
Treatment − Control ≥ Minimum Success Threshold
```

or

```text
Treatment − Control ≤ Minimum Success Threshold
```

depending on the business objective.

This provides a practical estimate of the likelihood that a customer or producer will observe sufficient value to justify product adoption. 

---

### 📊 Statistical Power Analysis

Calculate statistical power based on:

- Sample size
- Expected treatment effect
- Standard deviation
- Significance level (α)
- One-tailed or two-tailed testing

The application uses exact non-central t-distribution power calculations rather than simplified approximations. 

---

### 🎯 Sample Size Recommendations

Automatically estimates the required sample size per group for:

- 70% power
- 80% power
- 90% power

Supporting better trial planning and budget allocation. 

---

### 🔬 Multiple Endpoint Adjustment

Supports Bonferroni correction for studies with multiple primary endpoints.

Features include:

- Adjustable number of endpoints
- Dynamic α adjustment
- Corrected sample-size recommendations
- Corrected power calculations



---

### 🧠 Business-Focused Trial Design

Unlike conventional sample size calculators, Trial Navigator explicitly incorporates:

- Expected effect size
- Minimum commercially meaningful effect
- Variability
- Trial success probability

allowing teams to design trials that support both scientific rigor and business decision-making. 

---

### 📋 Reporting & Communication

Generate shareable summaries containing:

- Trial assumptions
- Power calculations
- Success probability
- Sample-size recommendations
- Correction settings

Ideal for:

- Research proposals
- Internal discussions
- Management reviews
- Customer-facing project planning

## Methodology

### Commercial Success Probability

The probability module estimates the likelihood that the observed treatment effect will exceed a predefined business success threshold.

```text
P(Observed Effect ≥ Business Target)
```

This helps quantify whether a trial is likely to create a compelling value proposition for customers and stakeholders. 

### Statistical Power

Power calculations are based on:

```text
Exact Non-Central t Distribution
```

and account for:

- Sample size
- Effect size
- Variability
- Tail selection
- Significance level
